About Me

Engineer by logic,
problem solver by instinct.

I'm a final-year Information Technology student at IIIT Gwalior with a CGPA of 8.02/10, graduating in 2027. My engineering philosophy is simple — a system is only as good as how it behaves when things go wrong, and I'd rather spend a day thinking through failure modes than a week debugging them in production.

That mindset is what pulled me toward System Design and core Software Engineering principles in the first place — distributed systems, fault tolerance, concurrency, consistent hashing. I actually enjoy sitting with a design problem, weighing trade-offs between five reasonable approaches, and defending why one is right for the constraints at hand. It's shaped my work across backend infrastructure, distributed systems, and applied ML — not because I'm chasing breadth for its own sake, but because the interesting problems rarely stay in one lane.

I also treat competitive programming as an ongoing practice rather than an interview checkbox — it's the clearest signal of how someone actually thinks. As GDG Tech Lead, I run a 200+ member developer community with Tier-1 pan-India status, mentoring students and organizing events that get people to actually build things.

Outside the screen: I captained an Under-17 National Football team, and I've carried the same instinct into every technical team since — stay calm under pressure, make the hard call early, and don't let people face the tough part alone.
